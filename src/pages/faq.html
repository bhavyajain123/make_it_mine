Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Make It Mine.

How do I customize a product?

Select a product from the Shop section and open the customizer. You can upload images, add text and choose product colors.

Can I save my design?

Yes. Designs can be saved so you can continue editing them later.

How can I track my order?

Open My Orders and select the Track Order option to view your order status.

What payment methods are available?

The checkout flow supports common payment options such as UPI, cards, net banking and cash on delivery.

Can I change my delivery address?

You can manage saved addresses from your Addresses section before placing an order.

How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page and send us your query.
